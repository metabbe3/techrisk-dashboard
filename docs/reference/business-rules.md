## Sub-incident roll-up (PROJ-022, owner-approved 2026-11-11)

A **super-incident** is a main incident with sub-incidents linked to it; a **sub-incident** is a separate full incident record whose `parent_incident_id` points at its super. One level deep (a sub can never have subs, and a linked record can't be linked again). Issues are untouched.

- **Counts roll up into the super.** Sub-incidents are excluded from every incident-COUNT surface: dashboard cards/charts (dimension charts roll subs into their supers), weekly counts, Reporting/SendReport listings, exports. Implemented as `countEligible(withSubIncidents: false)` / `aiCounts(withSubIncidents: false)` defaults + `withoutSubIncidents()` (`whereNull('parent_incident_id')`) — a new count surface MUST exclude subs.
- **Money stays with subs.** The Fund Loss tile/trend/Pareto keep counting sub fund loss — money surfaces opt in with `withSubIncidents: true`.
- **List:** subs are hidden unless expanded or surfaced — search, tabs, filters, `?ids=` drill-in all surface them; an expanded family sorts super→subs by adjacency. Issues/board are unaffected (board shows only chips).
- **Exports exclude subs entirely** (all 5 presets + Markdown ZIP) — via `baseExportScope()`; the **AI corpus keeps them** (knowledge base).
- **Reporting/SendReport mirror the dashboard** (subs rolled up, money kept).
- **Classification guard:** a record linked in a hierarchy (sub of a super, or a super with subs) cannot change classification Incident ↔ Issue until unlinked — edit-form rule, `downgrade_to_issue` and `bulk_downgrade` refuse with a notification. `hasIncidentLinks()` is the predicate.
- **Link semantics are MOVE:** `parent_incident_id` is a single column — linking a linked record means unlink first. Deleting a super orphans its subs (`nullOnDelete`), never deletes them.
- **Unlink never deletes:** every unlink path (list row action, view page, board) just sets `parent_incident_id = null`; both records stay.
- **Cache freshness:** `parent_incident_id` is in the observer's dirty-field trigger list — a link change recalcs metrics and bumps `dashboard_cache_version`.

## Outlier rule (owner 2026-10-01)

A Label `Outlier` tag removes the row from every MTBF/MTTR *average* (calculator nulls its stored metrics; every metric query/span composes `withoutOutliers()`; predecessor gaps telescope over it so Σgaps still equals span) while it stays in every *count* (Total Cases, status tabs, severity breakdowns, KPI counts, Group-By Cases) and every table/export row — its own MTTR/MTBF cells render the literal **`Outlier`** (`mttr_formatted` accessor + per-surface mtbf cells; live-table `state()` returns `int|string`). Tagging fires a recalc + cache bump via the `IncidentLabel` pivot — NOT the observer; raw pivot writes and deleting/renaming the label record bypass it (remedy: Recalculate button / sweep command).

## Status-update freshness (owner 2026-11-09/10)

- One shared rule on `Incident` feeding two surfaces so they cannot drift (BUG-005/006/007 lesson): the incident-list "Status update" column (`Incident::statusUpdateFlag()`) and the `reminders:send-incidents` status-update email lane (`Incident::statusUpdateReminderFlag()`, `StatusUpdateStaleReminder`).
- Rule: incident not `Completed` AND (no status update at all → "Need update", immediate on the list) OR last update's status not in `Resolved/Recovered/Closed` AND its `update_date` older than 6 days → "Needs follow-up".
- The EMAIL lane is the same rule except the empty case waits until the incident itself is also older than 6 days (owner 2026-11-10: the email waits — the screen nudge is immediate, the inbox nudge only for genuinely neglected incidents).
- Ordering for "last update" is `update_date DESC, id DESC` (`Incident::latestUpdateRecord()`), NOT the `latestStatusUpdate()` HasOne relation (that is latestOfMany by id — different ordering, used by Kanban/Reporting/etc.).
- Lane gated by the System Settings → Email tab toggle `status_update_reminder_enabled` + the shared `last_reminded_at` throttle and `netcore_enabled` kill-switch, like the other two reminder lanes. Delivery preference type: `status_update_reminder` (`User::getNotificationType()`).
- **Reminder emails are incident-only (owner 2026-11-18):** `SendIncidentReminders::dueIncidents()` filters `classification = Incident` for ALL three lanes (not-done, fund-loss, status-update). The table also holds `Issue` rows — including cases downgraded back from Incident (raised as issue → upgraded → failed the incident requirement → returned to issue) — and no lane may email or in-app-notify them; prod shipped the status-update reminder to such a row because nothing gated classification.

## Action Improvements lifecycle (PROJ-014, owner 2026-10-14)

- Statuses draft/pending/on_progress/done/cancelled/tbd; priority optional, default low.
- **Status `on_progress` (owner 2026-11-17, Rule A — "open work"):** accepted AND started, not finished. It rides every pending-gated surface — reminder emails, escalation, overdue tiles/counts, governance KPI, AI context, overdue filters, send-reminder action, badge (`info`/blue), and the observer's assignment mail (`notifyAssignees()` — rows created or PIC-edited as done/draft/cancelled/tbd never email their PICs, owner 2026-11-19). Every gate reads `ActionImprovement::openStatuses()` (pending + on_progress) — one shared list, never a hand-typed pair (BUG-005/006/007 rules 1/3). Only done/cancelled/tbd stop the chasing. Date-TBD semantics extend to on_progress rows: open with `due_date = null` = tracked (TBD rider, warning color), never chased. Draft and status-`tbd` stay parked.
- Legacy note: before 2026-11-17 the gates were `status = 'pending'` only — draft, tbd and cancelled rows dropped out automatically (tbd was verified free of charge: every gate already selected `status = 'pending'`, no gating code changed).
- Cancelling requires a `cancellation_reason` — enforced in the shared form only (`visible` + `required` when status = cancelled, the `result_note`/done mechanism: hidden Filament fields aren't validated). Deliberately NO observer validation, matching the `result_note` precedent; the write API routes stay disabled so the form is the only write path.
- Evidence: encrypted proof files (jpg/jpeg/png/webp/txt/pdf, ≤15MB) **and/or link rows** (`link_url` — chat / Notion URL, unencrypted, no key row) attachable anytime, one key per file (`ActionImprovementEvidenceKey`), ciphertext only on disk (`action-evidence/{uuid}.encrypted`). Evidence is immutable — no edit, delete + re-upload. File events `file_uploaded`/`file_deleted`/`file_downloaded` + link events `link_submitted`/`link_deleted` audit on the parent **Incident** (supporting-doc precedent), not on the evidence row; downloads gate through `ActionImprovementPolicy` view and REDIRECT to the URL for link rows (gate first — no open redirect for unauthorized users). Proof-workflow columns (owner 2026-11-20): `submitted_by`/`approved_by`/`approved_at` — `submitted_by` set + `approved_at` null = worker submission awaiting approval; plain Evidence-tab uploads have `submitted_by` NULL and never count as pending (`file_path`/`original_filename` are nullable for link-only rows).
- Transition INTO done stamps `completed_at`/`completed_by` in the observer (`??=` — editing a done row never re-stamps); reopening clears both.
- `registerCompletenessPct()` counts action-item EXISTENCE by design — a cancelled item still satisfies register completeness (no governance-number drift).
- The AI Suggest button only ever creates **draft** rows; drafts become real actions by promoting status to Pending.
- **TBD due dates (owner 2026-10-14):** `due_date = null` means TBD — every draft-creation path (AI Suggest, RCA parser/AI extractor, WarRoom retro) stores TBD when no real date is known, never an invented +30d; tables render `TBD`, and TBD rows are never due-soon/overdue/escalated (SQL null semantics, no code added). TBD rows are **tracked, not lost**: `tbd` filter on both tables, warning color on pending TBD, and the widget Pending card shows "N TBD (no due date)" — reminders skip them by design, so these surfaces are the only chase mechanism.

**Status `tbd` ≠ due-date TBD (naming hazard, owner 2026-10-26).** "TBD" already meant *no due date decided* (`due_date = null`); the 5th status `tbd` means something else entirely: *"TBD (to be discussed)"* — no decision yet on **whether/how** to do the action at all. A row can be either, both or neither independently (a `pending` row with a TBD date is chased; a `tbd`-status row with a real date is parked). Status `tbd` is parked exactly like draft/cancelled: gray badge, excluded from every pending gate (reminders, escalation, overdue, widget counts), and surfaces only via the status filter. All code and UI copy must keep the two distinct — constant `STATUS_TBD`, label "TBD (to be discussed)", never reuse the due-date "TBD" wording for the status.

## Incident Form Validation Rules (2026-09-29 hardening)

Live QA (HTTP-submit matrix) found bad data silently persisting; these constraints are now server-side and must not be removed:

- Fund fields (`potential_fund_loss`, `fund_loss`, `recovered_fund`): `numeric` + `minValue(0)` — negatives are data-entry errors.
- `incident_date`, `entry_date_tech_risk`: `maxDate(now())` — future dates are typos (wrong year) or clock skew.
- `discovered_at`, `stop_bleeding_at`: `afterOrEqual('incident_date')` — the timeline cannot precede the incident.
- Filament API note: `DateTimePicker` has **`maxDate()`**, not `maxValue()` (numeric/text only — using the wrong one throws 500).

## Investigation Form Intake — datetime rule (2026-11-17)

Every date/time field on the public intake is a native datetime-to-the-minute picker (`<input type="datetime-local" step="60">`, value `Y-m-d\TH:i`, WIB): `header.incident_date`, `header.statement_date`, `summary.occurred_time`, `summary.discovery_time`, `summary.stop_bleeding_time`, every chronology row's time, and every action item's due date (`empty = TBD`).

- **Never guess a missing date or minute.** AI output must be `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM` when the source gives both; anything else passes through untouched. `InvestigationFormSchema::toPickerValue()` is the single normalizer (accepts `./-`/space separators, drops seconds; a date OR minute missing/invalid → `null`, never filled in). Chronology times that can't be normalized ride the event text as `[source time: …]`; action due dates ride the item as `[source due date: …]`; "TBD" → empty.
- **Data precedence:** saved draft/manual edit > incident-record prefill > AI extraction. The AI merge fills BLANK fields only; the incident's non-nullable `incident_date` always covers `header.incident_date` + `summary.occurred_time`.
- **Submit gate:** non-canonical values (e.g. old free-text drafts like `2026.11.01 10:00`) are rejected on `consent` with the field list named — nothing is saved. Flat-field times pick with `max=now` (no future); due dates may be future.
- **Printing:** documents render `Y-m-d H:i` (the `T` is stripped via `InvestigationFormRenderer::display()`); an action row with an item but no due date prints `TBD`.

## Agent engines & chat reminders (unification, 2026-11-12)

- Every `AiAgent` has an `engine`: `go` (default — the DingTalk agent runs it as a tool-loop task via `POST /internal/run`, `X-Agent-Token` shared secret), `php` (single text-only generation, the pre-unification path, kept as fallback), `static` (no LLM — the instructions text is delivered as-is to the DingTalk target). All existing agents were flipped to `go` by the migration backfill (owner decision 2026-11-11); the engine branch lives **only** in `RunAiAgentJob::handle()` — `AiAgent::dispatchRun()` stays the single dispatch choke point.
- Chat-created reminders are `AiAgent` rows (the dashboard owns storage + dispatch; the Go side owns grammar validation and conversation binding). `mode: message` maps to engine `static`, `mode: task` to engine `go`; cron passes through verbatim, one-shots arrive as a resolved `run_at` (app timezone = WIB). Per-chat cap 20 (`short_no` = per-chat `#id`, unique `(dingtalk_conversation_id, short_no)`); cancel is soft (row kept, disabled, cron/run_at nulled) and only works from the owning conversation.
- One-shot (`frequency: Once`) lifecycle: fires when `run_at <= now && (last_run_at is null || last_run_at < run_at)`; success disables the agent; failure re-arms `run_at = now + 5min` while total failed runs < 3, else disables. A `Once` agent's `next_run_at` is `run_at` only while enabled + in the future.
- Failed-run retry: the `Retry` action on Agent Runs re-dispatches `agent->dispatchRun()`; `Run Now` stays the general entry point. Delivery failure (DingTalk push) fills `delivery_status`/`delivery_error` on the run but does **not** fail the run — the LLM output was produced.
- Turn reporting: every chat turn POSTs to `/api/v1/agent-runs` under the synthetic agent `00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000001` "DingTalk Bot (chat turns)" (`enabled=false`, never dispatched; `AGENT_TURN_REPORTS=0` silences). The synthetic agent's rows are excluded from Retry.
- Timeout ladder (all must stay ordered): Go run ctx 360s < HTTP 420s < job 480s < worker 660s ≤ redis `retry_after` 900s; lock TTL 900s.
- **Weekly digest recipe (no code — a scheduled-agent preset, 2026-11-18)**: in any chat, tell the bot "every Monday 9am, summarize last week's incident changes" — it creates a `schedule_reminder` with `mode: 'task'` and a prompt telling the run to `search_incidents` with `updated_since=<7 days before fire time>` and summarize status flips / new rows / fund-loss moves (the Reminders prompt carries a DAILY 24h variant of this recipe; scale the `updated_since` window for weekly). Delivered to the creating conversation; same rules as every reminder: per-chat cap 20, soft cancel from the owning conversation, visible + editable in the dashboard's AI Agents tab. Scheduled runs drop reminder/watch tickets and (when unbound from a conversation) the generate_* tools; **KB mutation tools (`save_knowledge`/`delete_knowledge`/`notion_add_database`) remain available to scheduled runs** — digest prompts are read-only by instruction, not by enforcement.

## Investigation form intake (PROJ-024, 2026-11-16; SSO-only 2026-11-20)

- **One form per team per incident** — `investigation_form_requests` unique (incident_id, team). The team shares ONE draft; **one submission** per request.
- **One link per person, SSO-only.** The admin picks recipients from the **directory user list** (searchable multi-select over `users` — name + email pairs, service accounts excluded; owner 2026-11-20: "can be from user list we have from all SSO"); each person gets their OWN link by email (`RequestInvestigationForm` — normalized lowercase emails, invalid rejected, capped at max recipients, enforced in the form via the Select's `maxItems`). The intake route carries `auth` middleware — a guest clicking the link goes **straight to Microsoft SSO** (`redirectGuestsTo` branch on `investigation-form.intake`) and returns to the exact URL via `url.intended`. A link is valid only when its recipient isn't revoked AND the request is pending + inside expiry — **submit, revoke or expiry kills every link at once**.
- **Identity is the signed-in user (owner 2026-11-20)** — `submitter_name`/`submitter_email`/`draft_updated_by_email`/notify email all come from `auth()->user()`; the step-1 name/email inputs are gone (job role stays editable, prefilled draft-first then the directory-synced `job_title`). **Forwarded links are fine** ("we can email to their lead but their lead ask their peer"): the token selects the request and checks validity, it does NOT authorize by recipient email — any tenant-authenticated identity may fill and submit, recorded under their OWN name. `MicrosoftLoginController` therefore logs in ANY Entra identity (JIT-created users land on their panel home — the incidents slice for the JIT `team-viewer` role, 2026-11-20 — or the intended URL; `canAccessPanel()` still denies `can_login=false` rows).
- **Tokens are derived, never rotated (owner 2026-11-20)** — `InvestigationFormRecipient::tokenFor()` = HMAC-SHA256 of `request_id|email` keyed by APP_KEY; `token_hash` remains the only stored value (sha256, "plain tokens never stored" holds). First send derives + hashes; every later send (Resend/Reissue/reminders) calls `linkToken()` → the SAME evergreen link, so nothing filled is ever orphaned by a reminder. `linkToken()` lazily re-keys pre-2026-11-20 rows (hashed from random tokens) at the next send. Bad/expired/revoked/submitted tokens all render the same generic invalid page (no oracle).
